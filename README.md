# AMS — Advanced Electronics Manufacturing Solutions

Welcome to the **AMS (Advanced Electronics Manufacturing Solutions)** Portal repository.

AMS is an independent standalone enterprise portal for India's Semiconductor & Advanced Electronics Ecosystem Partner, delivering end-to-end commercial solutions across manufacturing, packaging, academies, and national supply chains.

---

## 🚀 Overview

The AMS portal provides a dual-interface architecture:

1. **External View (Public Ecosystem Partner)**:
   - **Key Capabilities**: PCB Manufacturing Processes, Semiconductor Packaging, Customer Solutions Academy, Industry Leadership Academy, Learning Before Manufacturing, National Semiconductor Services.
   - **24-Month ERGI Roadmap**: USD 170M commercial progression and quarterly revenue mix across Early Revenue Generation (ERGI).
   - **28 States & 36 Trade Houses**: National distribution and trade execution network across India.
   - **Architecture & Ecosystem Integration**: Reference architectures for customer and sovereign partner deployment.

2. **Internal View (12-Leader Execution Intranet)**:
   - **Executive Roles & Mandates**: Structured governance across 12 leadership functions (CEO, CCO, CDIO, CSTO, COO, CTO, CQO, CPO, CAO, CFO, CLO, CHRO).
   - **Interactive Duty Profiles**: Role-based drill-downs with institutional mandates, delivery duties, and enterprise value metrics.
   - **Odisha Industrial Strategy**: 26-Chapter comprehensive industrial transformation and electronics manufacturing cluster roadmap.
